What is AQI?

AQI (Air Quality Index) is a standardized indicator of air pollutant levels. Higher values suggest poorer air quality and potential health impacts.

Data Source

Trend data shown here is indicative. For production, connect to reputable AQI feeds or local environmental agencies to populate the chart.

What you can do

Limit outdoor activity on high AQI days, close windows during pollution peaks, and use air purifiers where appropriate.
